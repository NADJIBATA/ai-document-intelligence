ANNUAL REPORT 2025

Highlights of the Year

Fiscal year 2025 was marked by strong growth and the launch of two new product lines. Revenue reached 184 million euros, an increase of 12.4 percent compared with 2024, driven by recurring software subscriptions. The customer base grew to 3,420 companies, and the gross retention rate remained above 94 percent. The group opened offices in Milan and Lisbon.

Financial Results

Operating income amounted to 27.6 million euros, giving an operating margin of 15 percent, up from 13.1 percent the previous year. Net income was 19.2 million euros. Free cash flow reached 31 million euros thanks to improved collection of receivables. The group ended the year with a net cash position of 58 million euros and no financial debt.

Revenue by Segment

Software subscriptions represented 71 percent of revenue and grew by 18 percent. Professional services accounted for 22 percent of revenue and declined slightly as customers adopted the self-service onboarding. Hardware sales, mainly network equipment, represented the remaining 7 percent. Europe generated 82 percent of revenue, North America 13 percent and the rest of the world 5 percent.

Workforce

At the end of 2025 the group employed 1,150 people, compared with 1,020 a year earlier. Research and development teams represent 38 percent of the workforce. Voluntary employee turnover decreased to 9.8 percent. Women represent 41 percent of employees and 36 percent of management positions, and the group aims for parity in management by 2030.

Environment

Greenhouse gas emissions on scopes 1 and 2 decreased by 21 percent compared with the 2022 baseline, mainly thanks to the move to renewable electricity in all offices. The group committed to a reduction of 42 percent of scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030. Data centres used for the hosted services are powered by electricity with guarantees of renewable origin.

Risks

The main risks identified are cybersecurity incidents, dependence on a limited number of cloud providers, the retention of key engineers and changes in data protection regulations. A dedicated risk committee reviews the risk map every quarter. Insurance policies cover cyber risks up to 25 million euros per year.

Outlook for 2026

For 2026, the group targets revenue growth between 10 and 13 percent and an operating margin of at least 16 percent. Investments will focus on artificial intelligence features, the expansion of the partner network in Southern Europe and the modernisation of the hosting infrastructure. The board will propose a dividend of 0.45 euro per share at the annual general meeting.
