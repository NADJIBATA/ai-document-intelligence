EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Working Hours

The standard working week is thirty-seven and a half hours, from Monday to Friday. Core hours run from 10:00 to 16:00; outside core hours, employees organise their schedule freely with their team. Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager and is compensated either by time off or by payment at the legal increased rate, at the employee's choice.

Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week once their probation period is completed. Remote days are agreed with the manager and recorded in the HR tool. The company provides a laptop, a second screen and a monthly remote work allowance of thirty euros. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours and use the company VPN to access internal systems.

Paid Leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to twenty-five days of paid leave per year, plus ten days of reduced working time. Leave requests are submitted at least four weeks in advance for periods longer than one week. Up to five unused days may be carried over to the following year; they must be taken before the end of March, otherwise they are lost.

Sick Leave

In case of illness, the employee informs the manager on the first day of absence and sends the medical certificate within forty-eight hours. From the first day of absence, the company maintains the full salary for up to ninety days per year, after deduction of social security daily allowances. Return-to-work meetings are offered after any absence longer than thirty days.

Training

Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 euros and five training days. Training requests are discussed during the annual review and validated by the manager. Certifications related to the job are fully paid by the company; if the employee leaves within twelve months after a certification costing more than 2,000 euros, a pro rata share of the cost may be claimed back.

Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed on presentation of receipts submitted through the expense tool within thirty days. Train travel is booked in second class for journeys under three hours. Meals during business trips are reimbursed up to twenty-five euros for lunch and thirty-five euros for dinner. Hotel nights are capped at 150 euros in Paris and 110 euros elsewhere.

Annual Review

Every employee has an annual review with the manager in January. The review covers the objectives of the past year, the skills assessment, the objectives for the coming year and the training plan. Salary reviews take place in March and are based on the assessment, the market position of the role and the company budget.
